Glow Maze Support

By 9Bit Studios

Need help with Glow Maze? Email 9bitdev@9bitstudio.com and include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the in-game Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I remove ads?
Buy Remove Ads in the in-game Shop. It is a one-time purchase.

How do I change my ad choices?
Open the in-game Settings and use the privacy options if they are shown. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.

My progress is missing.
Progress is saved on your device. Reinstalling the game or switching devices can reset it. Email us and we will try to help.

The ball feels wrong or the controls do not respond.
Glow Maze is controlled by tilting your device. Make sure Motion and Orientation access is not restricted and that Portrait Orientation Lock is off.
