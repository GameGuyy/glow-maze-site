Privacy Policy

Glow Maze by 9Bit Studios. Last updated: October 21, 2026.

This policy explains what information Glow Maze ("the app") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. We designed the app to collect as little as possible. You do not need to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous player ID and leaderboard data. The app signs you in anonymously (Unity Gaming Services) so you can appear on leaderboards. We store a random player ID, an automatically generated display name and your scores.
  • Gameplay and usage data. Events such as levels played, game modes, taps, session length, shop views and ad interactions, used to understand and improve the game.
  • Device and identifiers. Device model, operating system version, app version, language and install or device identifiers created by our analytics and ad providers. If you allow tracking on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) may be used for advertising.
  • Approximate location. Coarse location (country or region) inferred from your IP address. We do not collect precise location.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and performance data, so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Purchase events. When you buy something, Apple processes the payment. We receive confirmation of the purchase but never your payment card or Apple ID details.

How we use it

  • To run the game, save and show leaderboard rankings, and deliver purchases.
  • To measure and improve gameplay and performance, and to fix crashes.
  • To show ads and measure how they perform, which helps keep the game free.

Service providers

We use the following third-party services. Each collects data under its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Amplitude (analytics): amplitude.com/privacy
  • GameAnalytics (analytics): gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services (authentication and leaderboards): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple App Store (purchases): apple.com/legal/privacy

Advertising and your choices

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Where required by law (for example in the EU, UK and some US states) the app shows a consent message so you can choose how your data is used for ads. If a privacy options entry appears in the app's Settings, you can change your choice there at any time. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking, and reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Buying Remove Ads in the Shop removes ads from the game.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above, who process it for us, and when required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. To ask for your leaderboard entry or any data linked to your player ID to be deleted, email us at the address below with your device model and the approximate date you played. Local game progress is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Glow Maze is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, or to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
